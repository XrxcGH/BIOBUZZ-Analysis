## 5. What FIRST has said changes this season

### 5.1 The manual philosophy rewrite — the biggest story [C]

V0 §1.4.1 announces a deliberate break with how FTC manuals have been written:

> the prior approach focused on "covering exact circumstances and details, removing flexibility in favor of
> attempting to cover all scenarios"

BIOBUZZ instead "emphasizes the 'spirit of the rule' and empowers event volunteers to make good-faith
judgement calls," with rules written around **"intended gameplay"** so volunteers "watch for larger behaviors
and strategies" rather than record every incident. FIRST explicitly frames this as permanent — *"something we
plan to continue for future games and future seasons."*

**Why this matters for a loophole review:** a manual that trades specificity for intent is *simultaneously*
more loophole-resistant (referees can rule against the letter) and less predictable (identical strategies get
different calls at different events). The correct posture for BIOBUZZ is **not** "find the gap in the wording"
— §1.5.1 pre-commits every team to the opposite:

> "We Play the Game as Intended… Our team will not try to gain an advantage using any 'loopholes' in the
> rules. If there is ever ambiguity in the specific wording of a rule, we will look to the intent of the rule
> and spirit of the competition to guide our behavior."

Redirect the review toward **ambiguity that will cost you points at an event** (where two readings both look
legal and a ref may pick the other one) rather than exploitable gaps. Prepare Q&A submissions, not exploits.

### 5.2 Competition Integrity Contract (CIC) — new [C]

New V0 §1.5. Two halves: **Sporting Ethics Code** (mandatory) and **Behavior Guidelines**. Sporting Ethics
clauses: *We Don't Cheat · We Always Behave with Integrity · We Play the Game as Intended · We Know Our Team
Doesn't Enforce the Rules.* §1.5.3 covers Infractions, Mitigations & Escalation. Also new: a
**Framework of Behaviors** (§1.4.2) chaining Gracious Professionalism → Code of Conduct → CIC → Competition
Rules.

Practical consequence: "we understand teams need to abide by all the regulations, **even the ones that are not
checked**." Rules that inspection does not verify are still binding. [C]

### 5.6 Advancement [C]

BIOBUZZ V0 §4 is **final** (not a placeholder). Confirmed structure:

- Teams advance **only from events in their HOME REGION**; out-of-region play is not advancement-eligible.
- Advance from the **first three chronological entry-level events** (QT or LT). One League per season.
- Path: QT/LT → *(optional)* Super Qualifying Tournament (SQT, max one) → Regional Championship (RCMP) →
  *FIRST* Championship (FCMP) and/or *FIRST* Premier Event (FPE). Pilot events exist for new/emerging regions.
- PDPs set advancement counts up to RCMP; FIRST staff set RCMP → FCMP/FPE.
- §4.1 Advancement Points Calculation and §4.2 Advancement Distribution by Region are populated in V0.
- Region size snapshot for FCMP allocation: **2026-11-16**; last date for regions to be counted: **2026-08-17** (already passed).

Programme-level context [C, 2025-12-03]: FIRST introduced an Advancement Point model letting single-slot
regions advance on cumulative event performance (awards **and** robot performance); minimum 2 FCMP slots for
qualifying regions; remaining slots roughly proportional to region size; team preferencing runs through FTC
Scoring with assignments by mid-January. 2025-26 scale was 336 FCMP teams and 600+ opportunities across 12
Premier Events. Whether those numbers repeat for 2026-27 is **[U]** until allocations publish **2026-11-19**.

## 9. Traps, landmines, and things a student would miss

### 9.1 The September 4 early preview [C]

`2026-27_BIOBUZZ_ImportantSeasonDates.pdf` p.1 lists, between Aug 17 and Sep 11:

> **September 4, 2026 — Early Game Preview Information · Privacy Agreements Required for all Viewers**

This appears **nowhere** in any FIRST blog post and returned nothing in web search. Inference: a
volunteer/PDP/event-partner NDA briefing 8 days before public kickoff. [C for the line item, S for the audience]

**Consequences:** (a) between Sep 4 and Sep 12, some adults in your region will legitimately know the game and
be unable to say so — do not push them, and do not read their silence as signal; (b) leaked details may surface
on Discord/CD in that window and will be **unattributable and unverifiable** — treat anything that appears
between Sep 4 and Sep 12 as poisoned for the review harness.

### 9.2 The GATE red herring [C — resolved]

V0 §1.4.1 contains this example of the new enforcement philosophy:

> "…(e.g., strategically blocking an opponent from access to their gate vs. going into an opponent's gate zone)"

**This is not a BIOBUZZ field element.** GATE and GATE ZONE are **DECODE 2025-26** defined terms — DECODE §9.8.3
defines the GATE as a robot-activated push-to-open element on the CLASSIFIER ramp, and the GATE ZONE as a
2.75 x 10 in. taped volume adjacent to each GATE. Verified: `manuals/archive/2025-26_DECODE_Competition_Manual_TU32.txt`
lines 2243–2244 and 2358–2370. Note the V0 text uses **lowercase** "gate," while the manual's convention (§1.7.1)
is SMALL CAPS for defined terms — consistent with a prior-season illustration, not a BIOBUZZ definition.

**Anyone grepping V0 for leaked game vocabulary will hit this and get excited. It is the only such hit** —
`pollen`, `hive`, `nectar`, `flower`, `bee`, `honeycomb`, `bloom`, `petal`, `garden` all return zero matches in
the BIOBUZZ V0 text. Pre-empt it in the kickoff prompt.

### 9.3 Placeholder inventory [C]

Six placeholder blocks in V0 (`BIOBUZZ_V0_raw.txt` lines 1390, 1399, 1408, 1417, 2257, 2283) reading
*"This section will be updated with the Kickoff Competition Manual release on September 12, 2026"* — covering
**§8 Game Overview, §9 ARENA, §10 Game Details, §11 Game Rules (G), §13 Tournament (T), §15 *FIRST* Championship (C)**. ⚠️ **§14 League Play Tournaments (L) is NOT a placeholder** — it is fully written (V0 p.90), though it cross-references §13.6, Table 13-1 and §10.6.1, which do not exist yet.
Plus two in-line deferrals: **R105** ("Sizing Constraints and more details will be released at Kickoff") and a
live cross-reference to `GXXX` — a game rule that does not exist yet (line 1545). `963:XXX` is the other
unresolved token.

### 9.4 Date discrepancy [C]

The 2026-08-10 blog says the CM Preview released **July 29**. The CM Preview release blog is dated **July 31**,
and the V0 PDF's own creation timestamp is **2026-07-31 12:52:57 -04:00**. July 29 is when the FTC Events page
flipped to the BIOBUZZ season; July 31 is when the manual actually dropped. The Season Dates PDF (v26-27.1,
2026-06-24) says August 1 for both — it predates the actual release. **Trust the PDF metadata.**

### 9.5 Stale official pages [C]

As of 2026-08-22 the FTC game-and-season page still says *"Register for the **DECODE** season in the FIRST
Dashboard"* and offers *"Watch **DECODE** Game Animation."* The event resources page still says *"Structured
Interview Feedback Sheets"* though the manual renamed it Initial Interview. FIRST's web copy lags its documents;
when they disagree, **the manual wins**.

### 9.6 Q&A opens 16 days after kickoff [C]

Team Q&A opens **2026-09-28**, not on kickoff day. For the first 16 days the manual text is the only authority
and there is no mechanism to resolve ambiguity. That window is exactly when strategy gets locked in. Build the
ambiguity list in week 1 and file it on day one of Q&A.

### 9.7 Two other quiet ones

- **Servos 10 → 8** is a real design constraint that will bite mid-season, not at kickoff. Count servos in the
  first CAD review, not the first inspection. [C]
- **`event/travel-letter` became `event/transport-letter`** — slugs move between seasons. Any hard-coded URL
  carried over from the DECODE harness is suspect. [C]
